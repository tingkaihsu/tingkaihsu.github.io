Anomalous Threshold in Terms of Triple Cut

Scalar integral decomposistion:

For a one-loop amplitude of the two-to-two scattering of unstable scalar and spin-$\frac12$ fermion, we labels the in-out states of the scalar $1$ and $4$ while the in-out states of the fermion are $2$ and $3$.$$A_4(s,t) = \sum_{\Box_i} d_{\Box_i} I_{\Box_i} + \sum_{\triangleright_i}d_{\triangleright_i} I_{\triangleright_i} + \sum_{\triangleleft_i}d_{\triangleleft_i}I_{\triangleleft_i} + \cdots$$where the ellipsis represents the other scalar integrals and the rational term.

The topologies of the scalar integrals can be classified by the cuts.

Boxes

We can have four types of box topologies

  1. $\Box_1 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_1, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_3\}$,

  2. $\Box_2 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_1, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_4\}$,

  3. $\Box_3 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_3\}$,

  4. $\Box_4 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_4\}$.

Triangles

We can have four types of triangular topologies, each of them can correspond to two box topologies by including extra cut condition

  1. $\triangleright_1 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_1,\ell+p_1+p_2\}$ contains $\Box_1, \Box_2$,

  2. $\triangleright_2 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_4, \ell+p_3+p_4\}$ contains $\Box_1, \Box_3$,

  3. $\triangleleft_1 \equiv \{\ell,\ell+p_2,\ell+p_1+p_2\}$ contains $\Box_3, \Box_4$,

  4. $\triangleleft_2 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_3, \ell+p_3+p_4\}$ contains $\Box_2, \Box_4$.

其中$\triangleright_1$可以透過下列的變換得到其他的topologies

$$ \triangleright_1 \to \triangleright_2: p_1 \to p_4,\quad p_2\to p_3\,,\\ \triangleright_1 \to \triangleleft_1: p_1 \to p_2,\quad p_2 \to p_1\,,\\ \triangleright_1 \to \triangleleft_2: p_1 \to p_3,\quad p_2 \to p_4. $$In the following, we focus on the $\triangleright_1$ topology and the boxes $\Box_1$ and $\Box_2$ which it contains.

Double Discontinuities of Scalar Integrals

For the kinematics $t\to 0, s_2 = s_3^* = M_R^2-i \Gamma M_R$ and $s \in [s_{\triangleright}^+(s_2), s_{\triangleright}^-(s_2)]$, the double discontinuities of the scalar triangle

$$ \rm{Disc}_s^2 I_{\triangleright_1} = \frac{(2\pi i)^2}{\sqrt{s-(m-\sqrt{s_2})^2}\sqrt{s-(m+\sqrt{s_2})^2}}\,, $$

where the branch cut at $s\in [(m-\sqrt{s_2})^2, (m+\sqrt{s_2})^2]$.

The double discontinuity of the scalar boxes

$$ \rm{Disc}_s^2 I_{\Box_1} = \frac{(2\pi i)^2}{\sqrt{M^2(M^2-4m^2)}}\frac{1}{2i\Gamma M_R}\,, $$

and

$$ \rm{Disc}_s^2 I_{\Box_2} = \frac{(2\pi i)^2}{\sqrt{(s-s^{1}_{\Box_2})(s-s^{2}_{\Box_2})}\sqrt{(s-s^{3}_{\Box_2})(s-s^{4}_{\Box_2})}}\,, $$where $s^{i}_{\Box_2}$ are the solutions of $S_{\Box_2} = 0$. The branch cuts $s\in [s_{\Box_2}^1, s_{\Box_2}^2]$ and $s\in[s_{\Box_2}^3, s_{\Box_2}^4]$ are chosen to avoid the contour $s \in [s_{\triangleright}^+(s_2), s_{\triangleright}^-(s_2)]$. More precisely, up to $O(M^{-2})$, we have$$ [s_{\Box_2}^1, s_{\Box_2}^2] = m^2-2m\sqrt{s_2}+s_3+\frac{m(m\pm\sqrt{s_2})(s_2-s_3)}{M^2}\,, $$

$$ [s_{\Box_2}^3, s_{\Box_2}^4] = M^2+s_2-\frac{2i \Gamma M_R m^2}{M^2}, M^2+s_2+\frac{6i \Gamma M_R m^2}{M^2}. $$

I am still checking if the second branch cut could cut the contour…

The contour

$$ s_\triangleright^\pm = \frac{M^2}{m^2}\left(s_2\pm\sqrt{s_2(s_2-4m^2)}\right) + \mathcal{O}\left(M^0\right). $$To me, it seems that there is nothing stopping the triangle singularities to approach the box singularities.

Triple Cut Conditions

Let us consider the $\triangleright_1$-triple cut conditions$$ \ell^2 = M^2\,,\quad \ell_1^2 \equiv (\ell+p_1)^2 = m^2\,,\quad \ell^2_2 \equiv (\ell_1+p_2)^2 = m^2. $$Imposing these conditions, the loop momentum is left with an unfixed degree of freedom $z$
$$ \ell(z) = \alpha p_1 + \beta p_2 + r(\frac Qz + \bar Q z)\, $$where $\alpha, \beta, r^2$ are fixed by the cut conditions. Upon the triple cut, the loop integrand can be expressed in terms of a product of on-shell tree-level amplitudes attached to the cut intermediate states, and we define it as the triple-cut integrand$$ \mathrm{Cut}_{\triangleright_1}(z) = A_3(a) A_3(b) A_4(cd)\,, $$where $A_3(a) = A_3(\ell, p_1, \ell_1)$ and $A_4(cd) = A_4(\ell, \ell_2, p_3, p_4)$ are UV amplitudes since they are connected by the heavy state and $A_3(b) = A_3(\ell_1, \ell_2, p_2)$ is the IR amplitude. The spin degree of freedom of the intermediate states are summed over.

Box Coefficients

The UV amplitude $A_4(cd)$ can contain tree-level exchanges: the t-channel exchange is $\frac1{\ell_3(z)^2-m^2}$ where $\ell_3(z) \equiv \ell_2(z)-p_3$, the s-channel exchange is $z$-independent $\frac{1}{s-m^2}$, and the u-channel exchange is $\frac1{M^2+s_3+2m^2-\ell_3(z)^2-s-m^2}$.

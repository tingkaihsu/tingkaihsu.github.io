Large-M Expansion

重點在於：Plugging in the loop momentum solution $\ell(z) = \ell_{||} + \ell_{\perp}(z)$ does NOT enhance the M-power counting of the z-contour integral of the triple-cut integrand.

首先：從triple-cut conditions中可以得到平行p1-p2平面的loop momentum分量，以及transverse part的大小$\ell_{\perp}^2$。(已完成)

再來我們要去探討components的大小，我們選定C.O.M.座標其中$s = O(M^2)$和$t,s_2,s_3,m^2 = O(M^0)$並假設$p_1,p_2$在3-direction上反向，散射平面是along 1-3 direction。然後選定一個$Q,\bar Q$的向量基底，並提到loop momentum $\ell(z)$ is invariant under $Q\to \kappa Q, \bar Q\to \bar Q/\kappa, z\to \kappa z$。於是我們選擇$\kappa$使得 $|2p_3\cdot Q| = |2p_3\cdot \bar Q|$。因此，單位圓會區隔開兩個z-plane上的有限解，所以我們選擇它作為contour。

引進naive power counting for amplitudes：each UV three-point coupling is normalized by M，個別的mass dimension analysis。論述這個power counting其實是要對amplitudes product定義才是正確的：因為cut intermediate states carry complex momenta，所以wavefunctions存在$\rm{SL}(2,\mathbb{C})$的invariant以至於a single tree amplitude attached to cut spinning intermediate states (spinning states with complex momenta) is not bounded。但是當我們對切到的中間態自旋自由度求和時，這些rescaling factors會互相canceled。所以真正的問題在於：Whether plugging in the loop momentum solution $\ell(z)$ and evaluate the triple-cut integrand on the unit circle $|z| = 1$ enhance the M-power counting？結論是不會。首先the components of the transverse part are bounded by $|r| = O(M^0)$ on the unit circle。再來，the kinematic inputs to the IR three-point amplitude $\mathcal{A}_3(b) = \mathcal{A}_3(p_2, \ell_1, \ell_2)$:

$$\ell_1^\mu = \beta p_2^\mu + \ell_\perp^\mu + O(M^{-2})\,,$$

$$\ell_2^\mu = (1+\beta) p_1^\mu + \ell_\perp^\mu + O(M^{-2})\,.$$

are collinear with the external momentum $p_2$ up to $O(M^0)$。The large-M factors in these momenta $\ell_1, \ell_2, p_2$ can be viewed as a boost and thus does not change the naive power counting of the IR amplitude， 而the UV three-point tree amplitude $\mathcal{A}_3(a)$ 本身就有large-M scales from the attached heavy states。對於the UV four-point amplitude $\mathcal{A}_4(d)$來說我們還要考慮possible tree-level exchange at $\ell_3^2 = m^2$會不會帶來additional M-power，答案是在unit circle上 propagator $\frac{1}{\ell^2_3(z)-m^2}$有一個finite bound，with leading term $O(M^0)$。

所以我們有following inequality

$$\left| \oint_{|z| = 1} \frac{dz}{2\pi i z}\rm{Cut}_{\triangleright}(z) \right| \le \max_{|z| = 1} \left| \rm{Cut}_{\triangleright}(z) \right| = O(M^2)$$

Professor Advice

Professor does not like the choice of the contour at either $|z| = R$ or $|z| = 1$. His point of view is that the formula of double discontinuity$$ \rm{Disc}_s^2 A_4(s) = \rm{Disc}_s^2 I_{3}^{\triangleright} \oint \frac{dz}{2\pi i z} \rm{Cut}_\triangleright^{I_1I_4}(z) $$is a contour that catches the “correct” poles of box and triangle topologies. The circular contour is just a choice and therefore the large-M argument should not be proved by a particular choice of the contour. His suggestion is that the combination of the residues should commute with the large-$M$ expansion, i.e., like we found out in the $\ell\cdot \gamma$ expample, the unexpected large-$M$ terms cancel between two residues. He want to make the formula more precise and concrete on which box singularities should we choose to have the correct sign. Also, he think that add the U-channel pole in the four-point tree amplitude so that there are two box topologies and the simple circular contour might fails when there are many box singularities.

  1. Analyze how the $M$-dependence in the quadruple-cut solutions disappear in the combination of box and triangle residues, i.e., prove that the large-M expansion and taking the residues commute.

  2. Analyze a more general case where there is another box singularties present in the z-plane.

Anomalous Threshold in Terms of Triple Cut

Scalar integral decomposistion:

For a one-loop amplitude of the two-to-two scattering of unstable scalar and spin-$\frac12$ fermion, we labels the in-out states of the scalar $1$ and $4$ while the in-out states of the fermion are $2$ and $3$.$$A_4(s,t) = \sum_{\Box_i} d_{\Box_i} I_{\Box_i} + \sum_{\triangleright_i}d_{\triangleright_i} I_{\triangleright_i} + \sum_{\triangleleft_i}d_{\triangleleft_i}I_{\triangleleft_i} + \cdots$$where the ellipsis represents the other scalar integrals and the rational term.

The topologies of the scalar integrals can be classified by the cuts.

Boxes

We can have four types of box topologies

  1. $\Box_1 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_1, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_3\}$,

  2. $\Box_2 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_1, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_4\}$,

  3. $\Box_3 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_3\}$,

  4. $\Box_4 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2, \ell+p_1+p_2-p_4\}$.

Triangles

We can have four types of triangular topologies, each of them can correspond to two box topologies by including extra cut condition

  1. $\triangleright_1 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_1,\ell+p_1+p_2\}$ contains $\Box_1, \Box_2$,

  2. $\triangleright_2 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_4, \ell+p_3+p_4\}$ contains $\Box_1, \Box_3$,

  3. $\triangleleft_1 \equiv \{\ell,\ell+p_2,\ell+p_1+p_2\}$ contains $\Box_3, \Box_4$,

  4. $\triangleleft_2 \equiv \{\ell, \ell+p_3, \ell+p_3+p_4\}$ contains $\Box_2, \Box_4$.

其中$\triangleright_1$可以透過下列的變換得到其他的topologies

$$ \triangleright_1 \to \triangleright_2: p_1 \to p_4,\quad p_2\to p_3\,,\\ \triangleright_1 \to \triangleleft_1: p_1 \to p_2,\quad p_2 \to p_1\,,\\ \triangleright_1 \to \triangleleft_2: p_1 \to p_3,\quad p_2 \to p_4. $$In the following, we focus on the $\triangleright_1$ topology and the boxes $\Box_1$ and $\Box_2$ which it contains.

$$ \rm{Disc}_s^2 I_{\triangleright_1} = \frac{(2\pi i)^2}{\sqrt{s-(m-\sqrt{s_2})^2}\sqrt{s-(m+\sqrt{s_2})^2}}\,, $$
